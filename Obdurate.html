Artist statement

The word Obdurate means to stubbornly refuse to change one's opinion or course of action, even when faced with reason, persuasion, or pity. Those obdurate amongst us will find this quality to be their greatest source of pride but also their greatest source of pain. Mathematics, speedrunning, wisdom - these are all obdurate persuasions. The piece obdurate holds a candle this way through life, and takes a moment to sit with natural suffering which comes with obduration.

Orchestration: live drumming along to composed backing tracks. The video aspect shows various performance art collaborations.
