Revere

From Hamartia

“two stick figures which were in love but arguing.”

From my notes on an earlier version, September 2026.

The mood of two individuals is displayed through two coupled nonlinear stochastic differential equations. Affection, withdrawal and random disturbances shape the characters’ moods.

Watch the film ↗Listen ↗
About the mathematical model

The model is adapted from Alhaji Cherif and Kamal Barley’s 2009 paper, “Stochastic Nonlinear Dynamics of Interpersonal and Romantic Relationships.” This version retains its nonlinear relationship dynamics and uses independent, constant additive noise in place of the paper’s state-dependent noise. The parameters were chosen for this film.

A Metric Based on Insects

Music from Obdurate, 2021 · Film, 2026

One can always improve the precision of their measurements. So where does one draw the line? When one is trying to make absolute perfect analysis of that which they are deeply passionate, it is only natural to want to improve one's ability to measure. But what can you control? What can you count? What can you predict? What are the limits?

If one knew the exact positions, momentums, and spaces between chaotic swarms of unpredictable motions, could one finally understand that which they have been trying to understand for all their life? Is it inherently insane, or admirably brave, to attempt to control and define a metric as precise as one based on insects?

15 January 2021Album

Obdurate

The first album

Listen to Obdurate on Bandcamp ↗

“World building” is a common point of beauty between the art of progressive music and the art of video games. Inspired by this link, Obdurate features seven songs each of which defines its own world. A Metric Based on Insects, for example, follows a man lost in space who through his journey back to Earth comes to realise that he has fallen in love with the universe, but that this love is unrequited.

Each one of these worlds has associated to it a set of “hidden lyrics” which are not audible yet are intrinsically tied to the music.

On composing the music

Obdurate begins with a blank score. A grand staff, piano.

In exactly the same way, a good composer does not write metal because metal is cool, but instead a good composer allows the song to find itself, through patient listening.
