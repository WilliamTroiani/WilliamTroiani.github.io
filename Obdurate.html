Artist statement

Obdurate names a heightened form of stubbornness. A will set like stone. There is beauty in such resolve: mastery, rigor, and moral conviction often depend on the refusal to give in.

Yet stone does not bend. And when life demands movement, refusal becomes friction. Friction becomes dissonance. Dissonance becomes pain.

I have carried both the strength and the cost of this trait. With this piece, I invite others who have been both punished and rewarded by their own inflexibility to consider a shared question: what do we do when we are the Obdurate one?

In terms of orchestration: in the piece, mastery is represented by drum-kit performance, that of beauty in musical composition. The philosophy is carried by a mix of spoken word, poetry presented visually by video projection, and various other forms of obduracy are explored through various artistic collaborations.
